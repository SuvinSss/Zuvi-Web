Before uploading

  • Keep all 25 headers in the exact order. Optional values may be blank.
  • Required: store_code, external_sku, name, category_path, unit, unit_value, store_price.
  • Use exact existing category paths, separated by / . No new categories are created.
  • Units: PIECE, PACK, BOX, DOZEN, KG, GRAM, LITER, ML, METER.
  • Photos: up to 5 JPEG, PNG or WebP files per product, 5 MiB each. Exact flat filenames, including letter case; list from image_1 without gaps.
  • CSV: 5 MiB / 1,000 rows maximum. Prepared distinct image content: 2 GiB per job.
  • Blank opening_stock and opening_stock_reason create no stock movement. The sample leaves both blank.
  • Merchant requests must leave all four management-pricing values blank.

Sample values are illustrative. Replace them with verified data and your selected store code.
